# Best Brands — header & hero

Approved scope: a new website's header and hero only. Client approval precedes remaining homepage and other pages. Real-time Three.js/WebGL animation, not video. GSAP coordinates motion. No TURKA logo or branding.

Approved story: Enerjiden Güvene. Sunlight reaches solar cells, measured energy flow becomes road geometry, an inspection station and car convey precision and trust, ending in a shared infrastructure landscape. The visual relationship represents operational expertise, not a claim that stations are solar powered.

Latest approved copy: one heading only, bottom-left: Geleceğin altyapısına güç veriyoruz. No eyebrow, description, CTA buttons, lower chapter copy, captions or footer.

Navigation: Kurumsal, Enerji, Araç Muayene, Yatırımcı İlişkileri, Sürdürülebilirlik, İletişim, TR/EN.

Latest direction: full-bleed real-scale cinematic 3D flight. Sun, incoming sunlight and photovoltaic cells share the SAME physical shot; do not use a separate Sun photo, solar disk zoom or unrelated opening image. Raking sunlight arrives from the real HDR sun direction, cells respond, conductor flow collects in string inverters, enters the transformer/switchyard and propagates onto grid conductors. A free travelling measurement wave connects the end of production to the inspection laser while the camera flies continuously to the hall. No white-screen cut between sectors. No claim that this GES physically powers the station.

No procedural mountains, simple trees or floating platform. Photographic HDR distant environment and PBR asphalt/concrete. Detail the industrial electrical system, rather than only showing a transformer. GES control building carries Best Brands; the vehicle hall carries actual BESTE and ESBİ logos sourced from official WordPress media: /2026/06/BESTE-renkli-tagline.webp and /2026/06/ESBI-renkli-tagline.webp. No TURKA logo.

Brand colors sampled from original logo: dark #406840 and brighter #78b828. Original green logo on white header. One small heading bottom-left.

Playback: automatic on page ready, continuous 26-second base loop. User-approved interaction replaces click-to-pause: click the panel or “Akışa katıl” to enter a close flight following cells and under-panel DC collection. At the power system, choose “Dönüşümü incele” or “Şebekeye ilerle”; at inspection, choose “Ölçümü izle”. Each detour starts at the current camera pose, ends at the exact continuing film pose, and uses the same clock for camera and energy effects. Mouse position adds restrained, damped viewpoint parallax. Enter/Space activates the scene; hotspots and separate pause/resume/return controls are keyboard accessible. No charging-station scenario. Separate round pause control; no start gate. Slow flight for reduced-motion OS preference; CSS/photo fallback for unavailable WebGL.

Desktop only during this phase. Do not spend implementation or QA effort on mobile until the desktop experience is approved. Existing mobile styles are retained without new mobile work.

Environment polish: scanned CC0 Rock 07 asset, PBR terrain, undulating far ground, curved road curbs and fine verge grasses. No cartoon trees; distant vegetation comes from the photographic HDR environment. BESTE/ESBİ signs enlarged on a dedicated clear fascia; remove the front roof rail that crossed the logos. Electrical light and insulation share identical cable curves, photon endpoints derive from the tilted panel surface transform, and cell effects remain in the panel shader. These are explanatory visual effects, not a quantitative electrical simulation.
