# Best Brands — header & hero

Approved scope: a new website's header and hero only. Client approval precedes remaining homepage and other pages. Real-time Three.js/WebGL animation, not video. A single gated scene clock coordinates camera and effects. No TURKA logo or branding.

Approved story: Enerjiden Güvene. Sunlight reaches solar cells, measured energy flow becomes road geometry, an inspection station and car convey precision and trust, ending in a shared infrastructure landscape. The visual relationship represents operational expertise, not a claim that stations are solar powered.

Latest approved copy: one heading only, bottom-left: Geleceğin altyapısına güç veriyoruz. No eyebrow, marketing description, sales CTA, lower chapter copy, captions or footer. The user explicitly permits a contextual next-scene button and short click hint.

Navigation: Kurumsal, Enerji, Araç Muayene, Yatırımcı İlişkileri, Sürdürülebilirlik, İletişim, TR/EN.

Latest direction: full-bleed real-scale cinematic 3D flight. Sun, incoming sunlight and photovoltaic cells share the SAME physical shot; do not use a separate Sun photo, solar disk zoom or unrelated opening image. Raking sunlight arrives from the real HDR sun direction, cells respond, conductor flow collects in string inverters, enters the transformer/switchyard and propagates onto grid conductors. A free travelling measurement wave connects the end of production to the inspection laser while the camera flies continuously to the hall. No white-screen cut between sectors. No claim that this GES physically powers the station.

No procedural mountains, simple trees or floating platform. Photographic HDR distant environment and PBR asphalt/concrete. Detail the industrial electrical system, rather than only showing a transformer. No TURKA logo.

Playback (2026-10-29 correction): exactly THREE scenes, not an autoplay loop with optional detours. Scene 1 (Sun/PV) autoplays for 6.8 seconds and holds at film time 5.2.
