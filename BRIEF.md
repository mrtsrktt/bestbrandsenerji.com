# Best Brands — header & hero

Approved scope: a new website's header and hero only. Client approval precedes remaining homepage and other pages. Real-time Three.js/WebGL animation, not video. GSAP coordinates motion. No TURKA logo or branding.

Approved story: Enerjiden Güvene. Sunlight reaches solar cells, measured energy flow becomes road geometry, an inspection station and car convey precision and trust, ending in a shared infrastructure landscape. The visual relationship represents operational expertise, not a claim that stations are solar powered.

Approved copy:
- Eyebrow: Enerjiden ulaşıma, Türkiye’nin geleceği için
- Heading: Geleceğin altyapısına güç veriyoruz.
- Description: Yenilenebilir enerji üretimindeki deneyimimizi, yeni nesil araç muayene altyapısının güven ve teknoloji odaklı dönemine taşıyoruz.
- Primary CTA: Yatırımlarımızı Keşfedin
- Secondary CTA: Yatırımcı İlişkileri

Navigation: Kurumsal, Enerji, Araç Muayene, Yatırımcı İlişkileri, Sürdürülebilirlik, İletişim, TR/EN.

Art direction: deep forest/moss, warm ivory, lime used sparingly, sunrise amber; physically lit bespoke 3D geometry; editorial typography, restrained technical detail. Text always available. Reduced motion and WebGL fallback, adaptive resolution, mobile re-composition. Scene selection, pause/play and replay. No unsupported size/ranking claims.

Sources: https://bestbrandsenerji.com/ and https://bestbrandsenerji.com/portfolio/ . Existing logo preserved. The company's 2026-06-26 announcement describes 9 provinces / 14 stations as upcoming. No existing-operations claim.

Delivery: reviewable local development preview, production build and an independent private Sites review deployment. Do not replace the existing live company site.
